# Play Health apps declaration draft

12 October 2026. Complete this under Play Console → Policy → App content → Health apps, for the same AAB as [PLAY_DATA_SAFETY.md](PLAY_DATA_SAFETY.md). Do not select "The app does not have any health features."

Chisel is a cosmetic and educational app. It is not a medical device, not a clinical service, and it does not use Health Connect. The features below are still health-related features under Google's form.

## Select these

### Activity and fitness

Select **Activity and fitness**.

The app includes face and neck movement routines, posture practice, adaptive 30/60/90-day routine check-offs, and optional massage guidance. It can record which of those actions the person marked done, on this device.

It does not read heart rate, steps, GPS routes, weight, or body composition. Camera cues are approximate form hints. They do not measure pressure, blood flow, muscle activation, fat loss, or bone change. The in-app safety copy tells the person to stop for pain, clicking, locking, dizziness, numbness, or discomfort.

### Stress management, relaxation, mental acuity

Select **Stress management, relaxation, mental acuity**.

The app includes affirmations and a short meditation / visualization screen. This is general relaxation content. It is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis care, or a mental-health treatment. Do not also select **Mental and behavioral health**.

### Sleep management

Select **Sleep management**.

The person can type a sleep duration (hours). Chisel stores that number on the device and may mention it next to skin or under-eye notes. It does not sense sleep, use the microphone, or read another app's sleep data.

### Nutrition and weight management

Select **Nutrition and weight management**.

The person can type a water volume and a coarse salt level (low / mid / high). Those values stay on the device and are used only as context for facial-puffiness notes. There is no weight log, calorie log, diet plan, or weight-loss claim. If the console's expanded description of this category is limited to weight-management products and clearly excludes a water/salt note, leave this box unchecked and say so in the review notes. The default for this source is to select it, because the app records those nutrition-related inputs.

## Do not select

- Period tracking
- Diseases and conditions management
- Clinical decision support
- Disease prevention and public health
- Emergency and first aid
- Healthcare services and management
- Medical device apps
- Mental and behavioral health
- Medical reference and education
- Medication and treatment management
- Physical therapy and rehabilitation
- Reproductive and sexual health
- Human subjects research
- Other
- The app does not have any health features

Massage and face routines are wellness guidance, not physical therapy. Affirmations are not a mental-health service.
