Chisel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Chisel ("the app", "we") is a facial self-improvement app: on-device facial analysis, grooming and makeup guidance, affirmations and progress tracking. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: almost everything stays on your device, and we don't run ads, analytics, or trackers.

Who we are

Developer contact: nittureddy26@gmail.com. Chisel is intended for users aged 17 and over and is not directed to children.

Data processed on your device only

Your camera feed, facial measurements, scan history, progress photos, makeup looks, routines, streaks, sleep/water/salt logs, and identity setting are processed and stored locally on your device (in the app's local storage). They are not uploaded to us and are not visible to us.

Data sent off your device

  • Photoreal render (optional). If you use the optional "Make it photoreal" / photoreal features, the current photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our backend, which forwards it to a third-party image-generation provider (Replicate) to produce the result. The image is used only to generate that result and is not retained by us after the response is returned.
  • Device identifier. A random identifier generated on your device is sent with render requests so we can apply free-usage limits and prevent abuse. It is not linked to your name, email, or real-world identity.
  • Purchases. If you buy a subscription or trial, the transaction is handled by Google Play Billing (via RevenueCat for entitlement management). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

What we do NOT collect

No account or login. No advertising. No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. No contacts, no location, no background data collection.

How data is shared

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only as described above: the photo you submit for a photoreal render is shared with our image-processing provider for that purpose, and purchase data is handled by Google Play / RevenueCat.

Data retention & deletion

On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings. On our backend we keep only a per-device usage counter and (for subscribers) an entitlement record; you can request deletion of these by emailing nittureddy26@gmail.com, and we will delete them except where retention is required for fraud prevention or legal reasons.

Security

Data sent off the device is transmitted over TLS (HTTPS). Because the app has no login, most of your information never leaves your device at all.

Permissions

Camera — required for live facial analysis, affirmations, try-on and makeup features. The camera is used only while you are in those screens.

Medical disclaimer

Chisel provides cosmetic and educational guidance only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. For skin, hair, dental or health concerns, consult a qualified professional.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
