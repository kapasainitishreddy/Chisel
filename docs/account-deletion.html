Delete your Chisel data

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This is the public request page for the free internal-test release of Chisel. It explains how to remove data the app stored, and how to ask for deletion of an existing cloud account. This release does not create new accounts and does not sell credits.

Data stored only on your phone

Open Chisel, go to the Connect tab, and choose Clear all my data. That removes Chisel's local copies of scans, progress photos, saved looks, routines, streaks, and settings. It does not delete the original photo in your gallery, an encrypted backup file you already saved or shared, or a copy you sent to someone else.

Uninstalling Chisel, or clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, also removes the on-device app data.

An existing cloud account

New paid accounts stay off in this release. If you already have a cloud account from an earlier build, open Home, choose Progress reports & private tools, then Manage existing cloud account. That screen can request an email code for an existing account. It does not create an account. Delete cloud account asks the account-lifecycle service to remove the account and its generated server outputs.

That service is implemented in the app source and is not deployed for this internal-test build, so the in-app button cannot finish a live cloud deletion until the developer deploys and accepts it. Until then, email nittureddy26@gmail.com from the account address with the subject Chisel account deletion. Include the email address on the account. We will delete the cloud account, generated outputs, and associated profile data, except records kept for fraud prevention, tax, or another legal duty. We do not have your payment card number.

What this page does not delete

  • Photos that remain in your phone's gallery.
  • Encrypted backup files or discussion reports you downloaded or shared.
  • Copies held by an image provider after you chose optional cloud photo editing. Provider retention is separate and is not completed by this page.
